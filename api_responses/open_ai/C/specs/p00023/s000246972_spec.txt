1. **Functional Description**  
The program determines the spatial relationship between two circles based on their centers and radii, returning a classification of their interaction.

2. **Inputs**  
- An integer representing the number of datasets.  
- For each dataset, two sets of three floating-point numbers representing the x-coordinate, y-coordinate, and radius of two circles.

3. **Outputs**  
- For each dataset, an integer indicating the relationship between the two circles:  
  - 0: Circles are separate.  
  - 1: Circles intersect.  
  - 2: Circles are identical.  
  - -2: One circle is contained within the other.

4. **Preconditions**  
- The number of datasets is a non-negative integer.  
- Each circle's radius is a non-negative floating-point number.

5. **Postconditions**  
- The output integer accurately reflects the relationship between the two circles based on their positions and sizes.

6. **Invariants**  
- The program maintains the integrity of the input data throughout its execution.  
- The relationship classification is consistent with the geometric properties of circles.

7. **Error Handling / Exceptional Behavior**  
- Not inferable from code.

8. **State Transitions (if applicable)**  
- The program transitions through states based on the number of datasets processed, updating the relationship classification for each pair of circles.

9. **Non-functional Constraints (if inferable)**  
- The program operates within a time complexity that is linear with respect to the number of datasets.  
- The output is produced in a format suitable for standard output.
