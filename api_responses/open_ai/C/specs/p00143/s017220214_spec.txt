1. **Functional Description**  
Determines whether two points are on the same side of the edges of a triangle defined by three other points. It processes multiple test cases and outputs a result for each case.

2. **Inputs**  
- An integer representing the number of test cases.  
- For each test case, eight integers representing the coordinates of three points defining a triangle and two additional points.

3. **Outputs**  
- For each test case, outputs "OK" if one of the additional points is on the same side of the triangle as the other, otherwise outputs "NG".

4. **Preconditions**  
- The number of test cases is a non-negative integer.  
- The coordinates provided are valid integers.

5. **Postconditions**  
- The output for each test case correctly reflects whether the two additional points are on the same side of the triangle defined by the three points.

6. **Invariants**  
- The triangle defined by the three points is consistently referenced across all checks within a test case.  
- The relationship between the points is maintained throughout the evaluation of each test case.
