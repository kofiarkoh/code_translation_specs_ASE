- \( N \) strings of length \( N \) representing the initial state of the grid.  
- For each of the \( C-1 \) operations, an integer \( k \) followed by \( k \) pairs of integers \( (x, y) \) indicating the coordinates to be toggled.

3. **Outputs**  
- An integer representing the total count of conditions met after all updates.
