1. Functional Description  
Calculates the largest integer that can be expressed as a product of prime factors of a given number, raised to a power determined by the count of those factors, constrained by a specified threshold.

2. Inputs  
Two long integers representing a number and a threshold.

3. Outputs  
A long integer representing the computed product of prime factors, printed as a side effect.

4. Preconditions  
The first input must be a non-negative integer. The second input must be a positive integer.

5. Postconditions  
The output is the largest integer that can be formed by multiplying prime factors of the second input, each raised to a power determined by the count of those factors divided by the first input.

6. Invariants  
The computed product will always be a non-negative integer. The output will be zero if no valid prime factors meet the specified conditions.

7. Error Handling / Exceptional Behavior  
If the inputs do not meet the preconditions, the behavior is not defined. No explicit error handling is implemented.

8. State Transitions (if applicable)  
The state transitions involve the modification of the product based on the prime factorization of the second input and the count of those factors relative to the first input.

9. Non-functional Constraints (if inferable)  
The computation is expected to complete in a time complexity that is dependent on the square root of the second input, with space complexity being constant.
