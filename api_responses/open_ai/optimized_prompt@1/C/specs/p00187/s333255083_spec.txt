1. **Functional Description**  
The code determines the area of a triangle formed by three pairs of coordinates and categorizes the area into specific classifications based on its size.

2. **Inputs**  
The program reads multiple sets of four integer values representing the coordinates of two line segments, repeated until the end of input.

3. **Outputs**  
The program outputs a classification string based on the computed area of the triangle formed by the intersection of the line segments. Possible outputs include "kyo", "syo-kichi", "kichi", "chu-kichi", and "dai-kichi".

4. **Preconditions**  
The input must consist of valid integer values representing coordinates. The program expects at least three sets of coordinates to perform its calculations.

5. **Postconditions**  
After processing three sets of coordinates, the program will output a classification based on the computed area of the triangle formed by the intersections of the line segments. If no valid triangle can be formed, it outputs "kyo".

The program maintains that the coordinates are always integers and that the area calculations are based on valid geometric principles. The classification is determined solely based on the computed area.

If the input does not provide enough data to form three sets of coordinates, the program will not produce any output. If the line segments do not intersect to form a triangle, the output will be "kyo".

8. **State Transitions (if applicable)**  
The program transitions between states based on the number of coordinate sets processed. It resets after processing three sets, ready to accept new input.
