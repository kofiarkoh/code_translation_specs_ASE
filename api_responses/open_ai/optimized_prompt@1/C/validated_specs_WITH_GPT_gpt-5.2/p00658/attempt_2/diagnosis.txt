B. What the current specification may be missing or misstating  
- **Grid symbols are incorrect/underspecified in the spec-derived implementation.** The source uses:
  - Player start: `'S'` (not `'P'`)
  - Empty: `'.'`
  - Goal: `'G'`
  - Hole: `'B'` (not `'H'`)
  - Walls/solids: `'#'`
