# Revised Specification

## 1. Functional Description
The code determines the area of a triangle formed by three pairs of coordinates and classifies the area into specific categories based on its size. It also checks for intersections between line segments defined by the coordinate pairs. If the line segments are collinear or overlapping, the program will classify the area as "kyo" without further area calculation.

## 2. Inputs
- Six pairs of integers representing coordinates: (ax, ay), (bx, by), (cx, cy), (dx, dy) for line segments.
- The input is read until three sets of coordinates are provided.

## 3. Outputs
- A classification of the area of the triangle formed by the three sets of coordinates, which can be one of the following: "kyo", "syo-kichi", "kichi", "chu-kichi", or "dai-kichi".

## 4. Preconditions
- The input must consist of valid integer pairs representing coordinates.
- Exactly three sets of coordinates must be provided for processing.

## 5. Postconditions
- If the line segments intersect, the area is calculated and classified.
- If the line segments do not intersect or are collinear/overlapping, the output is "kyo".

## 6. Invariants
- The area calculation is based on the coordinates provided and remains consistent across valid inputs.
- The classification of the area is determined solely by the computed area value.

## 7. Error Handling / Exceptional Behavior
- If the input does not consist of three complete sets of coordinates, the program will not produce a valid output.
- If the area calculation results in a value that cannot be classified, the output defaults to "kyo".
- The program does not handle cases where line segments are collinear or overlapping in a manner that would yield a non-zero area; such cases will also result in the output "kyo".

## 8. State Transitions (if applicable)
- The system transitions from reading input to processing the coordinates and finally to outputting the classification based on the area.

## 9. Non-functional Constraints (if inferable)
- The program must handle input efficiently and provide output in a timely manner, though specific performance metrics are not defined.
