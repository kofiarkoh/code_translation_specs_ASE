The program simulates the movement of an entity in a 2D space based on specified mirror placements, determining the final coordinates after a series of movements.

- A non-negative integer representing the number of mirrors to be placed.  
- For each mirror, a string indicating the orientation ('x' for vertical, any other character for horizontal) and two integers representing the coordinates of the mirror's position.

3. **Outputs**  
- The final coordinates of the entity after processing the movements, printed as two integers.

4. **Preconditions**  
- The input integer must be non-negative.  
- The coordinates provided for mirrors must be within the defined bounds of the simulation space.

- The output coordinates reflect the final position of the entity after all movements and interactions with mirrors have been processed.

- The entity's position remains within the defined simulation space unless a wall condition is triggered.
