1. **Functional Description**  
The program simulates a battery management system that processes a series of battery usage and recharge cycles over a specified time limit. It calculates the total number of times batteries are used without being recharged during the given time frame.

2. **Inputs**  
- A positive integer representing the number of batteries.  
- A positive integer representing the time limit for the simulation.  
- For each battery, a sequence of positive integers representing usage times followed by corresponding recharge times.

3. **Outputs**  
- A single integer representing the total count of battery usages that occurred without a recharge during the specified time limit.

4. **Preconditions**  
- The number of batteries must be greater than zero.  
- The time limit must be a positive integer.  
- Each battery must have at least one usage and recharge time specified.

5. **Postconditions**  
- The output integer reflects the total number of battery usages that occurred without a recharge within the time limit.

6. **Invariants**  
- The state of each battery (active or inactive) is maintained throughout the simulation.  
- The queue of batteries awaiting recharge is consistently updated based on their usage and recharge cycles.

7. **Error Handling / Exceptional Behavior**  
- If the input for the number of batteries or time limit is non-positive, the program terminates without processing further.  
- If the input for battery usage or recharge times is non-positive, it is ignored for that battery.

8. **State Transitions (if applicable)**  
- A battery transitions from active to inactive when its remaining time reaches zero.  
- A battery transitions from inactive to active when it is recharged, based on the queue of batteries.

9. **Non-functional Constraints (if inferable)**  
- The program operates within a fixed memory limit defined by the maximum queue size.  
- The execution time is bounded by the product of the number of batteries and the time limit.
