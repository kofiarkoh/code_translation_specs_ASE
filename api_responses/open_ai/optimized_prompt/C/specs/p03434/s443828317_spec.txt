1. **Functional Description**  
The program reads a sequence of integers, sorts them, and computes the difference between the sum of the largest half and the sum of the smallest half of the sorted integers.

2. **Inputs**  
- An integer \( N \) representing the number of elements in the sequence.  
- A sequence of \( N \) integers.

3. **Outputs**  
- A single integer representing the difference between the sum of the largest half and the sum of the smallest half of the sorted integers.

4. **Preconditions**  
- \( N \) is a positive integer.  
- The sequence contains \( N \) integers.

5. **Postconditions**  
- The output is the computed difference as described in the functional description.  
- The sequence of integers is sorted in non-decreasing order prior to the computation of sums.

6. **Invariants**  
- The sequence remains unchanged in terms of its elements, but is sorted before the sums are calculated.  
- The sums \( A \) and \( B \) are computed based on the sorted sequence.

7. **Error Handling / Exceptional Behavior**  
- If \( N \) is less than or equal to zero, the behavior is not defined.  
- Memory allocation failures during sorting may lead to undefined behavior.

8. **State Transitions (if applicable)**  
- The state transitions from an unsorted array of integers to a sorted array, followed by the computation of sums and the final output.

9. **Non-functional Constraints (if inferable)**  
- The time complexity is \( O(N \log N) \) due to the sorting operation.  
- The space complexity is \( O(N) \) due to the additional storage used for sorting.
