1. **Functional Description**  
The program computes the circumcenter and circumradius of a triangle defined by three points in a 2D space. It processes multiple sets of triangle vertices as specified by the user.

2. **Inputs**  
- An integer indicating the number of triangle vertex sets.  
- For each set, six floating-point numbers representing the coordinates of three points in 2D space.

3. **Outputs**  
- For each triangle, the program outputs three floating-point numbers: the x-coordinate of the circumcenter, the y-coordinate of the circumcenter, and the circumradius, formatted to three decimal places.

4. **Preconditions**  
- The input integer must be a non-negative value.  
- Each set of six floating-point numbers must represent valid coordinates in a 2D space.

5. **Postconditions**  
- The output will consist of three floating-point numbers for each triangle, representing the circumcenter and circumradius, provided the input is valid.

6. **Invariants**  
- The circumcenter and circumradius are computed based on the geometric properties of the triangle formed by the three input points.  
- The circumradius is always non-negative.

7. **Error Handling / Exceptional Behavior**  
- If the input does not conform to the expected format or range, the behavior is not defined.  
- Division by zero is avoided through the mathematical formulation, but if the points are collinear, the circumcenter calculation may yield undefined behavior.

8. **State Transitions (if applicable)**  
Not applicable.

9. **Non-functional Constraints (if inferable)**  
- The program guarantees that the output is formatted to three decimal places.  
- The execution time is dependent on the number of triangle vertex sets processed.
